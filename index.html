What We Teach

Core Subjects

We offer in‑depth tutoring in three key subjects, following the Ontario curriculum and beyond. Each course is tailored to the student's level and goals.

🔬

Physics

From kinematics to quantum mechanics, our physics tutors break down complex theories into understandable concepts. We cover:

  • Grade 7‑12 Ontario curriculum
  • IB / AP Physics preparation
  • College & university intro physics
  • Exam practice & lab report support
📐

Maths

Build confidence and problem‑solving skills in algebra, calculus, geometry, and statistics. Our approach includes:

  • Foundations to advanced functions
  • SAT / GMAT quantitative prep
  • Homework help & test strategies
  • Real‑world applications
📖

Amharic

Whether you're a heritage speaker or a complete beginner, our Amharic lessons focus on reading, writing, grammar, and conversation.

  • Alphabet & pronunciation
  • Literature & essay writing
  • Speaking & listening practice
  • Cultural context & history
